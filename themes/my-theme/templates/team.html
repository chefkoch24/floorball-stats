Stat Guide

Quick explanation of the most important team metrics on this page.

  • Points/Game: Average standings points won per match.
  • Goals/Game: Average goals scored per match.
  • Goals Against/Game: Average goals conceded per match.
  • Goal Diff/Game: Average scoring margin per match (goals for minus goals against).
  • Powerplay %: Share of power plays converted into goals.
  • Boxplay %: Share of penalty kills without conceding.
  • Scoring Ratio: Goals for divided by goals against.
  • Close Game: Games decided by a small margin (typically 1-2 goals or OT/PS).
