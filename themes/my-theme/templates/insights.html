How the power ranking works
  • Rating is an Elo rating: every game moves points from the loser to the winner, more for a surprise and for a big margin, less for a win after overtime or penalty shots. 1500 is an average team, the home team gets a bonus of 45 points.
  • Ratings carry over between seasons and move a quarter of the way back to 1500 in summer. Newly promoted teams start at 1450.
  • 7 days is the rating change over the last week of games, the small number next to the place the change in the ranking.
    • {% if power.simulated %}
  • Proj. pts, 1st, Top 4{% if power.places %}, Playoffs{% endif %}{% if power.has_title %}, Title{% endif %} come from playing the rest of the season {{ '{:,}'.format(power.simulations) }} times with these ratings plus some uncertainty about each team's true strength{% if power.has_title %}, then the playoff bracket with the best seed against the worst remaining one in every round{% endif %}.
    • {% endif %}
  • Tested on the 25-26 and 26-27 games of all leagues: teams given a 70 to 80% chance won 74% of those games.
