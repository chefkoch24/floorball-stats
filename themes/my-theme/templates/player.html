Stat Guide

Player metrics are cumulative totals and per-game rates for the selected season scope.

  • Games: Matches the player appeared in.
  • Goals / Assists / Points: Core offensive totals (`Points = Goals + Assists`).
  • PIM: Penalty minutes taken by the player.
  • Points/Game: Offensive production rate per appearance.
    • {% if article.current_season_is_tournament != 'yes' %}
  • Regular vs Playoffs: Same season split by phase to show context changes.
    • {% else %}
  • Tournament Season: Tournament appearances are combined into one season total.
    • {% endif %}
  • Season History: Archive of season-by-season totals across leagues/phases.
  • Game-changing goals: Goals that tied the game (equalizers) or gave the lead (go-ahead goals), at any time.
  • Clutch points: Game-changing goals in the last ten minutes of regulation or in overtime, plus assists on them. Every overtime goal counts.
  • Game winners (GWG): The goal that gave the winner one more goal than the loser's final total (none after penalty shots).
  • Compare with another player: Opens the player comparison with this player chosen. Pick a second player to see both side by side for the latest season and the career; per-game values are the fair comparison across leagues (how it is counted).
  • PP / SH goals: Goals scored with a man up (powerplay) or a man down (shorthanded) in the games we track. A powerplay goal ends the opponent's minor penalty.
  • Scoring partners: Teammates this player scored from or set up most often this season.
