Summary: Floorball Connect brings together what the Floorball community already knows: reviews, drills, guides, where to watch and stats, in one place.

Floorball is a fast-growing sport that still doesn't get the attention it deserves.

In our view, the main challenge is not a lack of passion or content, but fragmentation. Information is spread across social media, forums, club websites, private chats, podcasts and different languages. Valuable insights get lost in feeds, are difficult to compare, or never reach the people who could benefit from them.

This project starts from that observation. Our goal is to connect what already exists and make it easier to discover, understand and build upon.

We are active ourselves as players, coaches and fans, and this project is built on hands-on experience. By combining different sources and perspectives, we want to create more clarity, structure and accessibility for the Floorball community.

## What we're building

### A connected Floorball world

At its core, Floorball Connect aims to bring people closer together. Players, coaches, clubs, fans and creators should be able to find each other's knowledge more easily and learn across borders, leagues and levels.

### Ratings and reviews

Equipment choices matter, but honest and comparable information is often missing. Floorball Connect introduces a structured way for players to share real ratings and reviews of sticks and blades, based on experience rather than advertising. The goal is transparency and better decision-making for everyone.

### Content and insights

There is already a lot of great Floorball content out there. We focus on curating, organising and highlighting it, from training drills and player development tips to podcasts, interviews and long-form insights. The aim is to make learning easier and more accessible.

### Data, stats and analytics

[Floorball Stats](https://stats.floorballconnect.com/) follows the top leagues in Europe, men and women: standings, results, player stats, power rankings and the story of every game, in one place.

## Open for collaborations

Collaboration is a fundamental part of who we are. If you're a player, coach, club, content creator, developer, or simply someone with a thoughtful idea, you're welcome to get involved. This can mean contributing content, sharing feedback, building something together, or just exchanging perspectives.

Floorball grows when people share what they know, when experiences don't stay isolated, and when learning becomes easier across borders and levels. Floorball Connect is a small step in that direction.
